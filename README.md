## 🌷 1. Taxi Driver
![taxi driver poster](https://images6.fanpop.com/image/photos/43800000/Taxi-Driver-Poster-korean-dramas-43854276-850-1201.jpg)

### Genre: Action · Crime · Thriller

*Information:*  
*Taxi Driver* follows a secret taxi company that takes revenge for victims when justice fails. It exposes real issues like scams, corruption, and bullying in society. Each case is emotional and deeply satisfying because the criminals are punished. It shows how justice can exist outside the system when the system is broken.

*Main Cast:* Lee Je-hoon · Kim Eui-sung · Pyo Ye-jin  

*📺 Episodes:* S1 — 16 | S2 — 16

## 🌷 2. Sweet Home
![sweet home poster](https://0.soompi.io/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/02174407/Sweet-Home.jpeg)

### Genre: Horror · Thriller · Apocalyptic

*Information:*  
*Sweet Home* is about people living in an apartment building who face a strange event — humans start turning into monsters. I find it interesting because it’s not just about survival, but also about facing inner fears and pain. The story shows how people change when they’re scared but still try to protect each other. It’s exciting and emotional, which makes it more meaningful and enjoyable to watch.
*Main Cast:* Song Kang · Lee Jin-wook · Lee Si-young  

*📺 Episodes:* S1 — 10 | S2 — 8 | S3 — 8  

---

## 🌷 3. Nine Puzzle
![nine puzzel poster](https://i.redd.it/f9bqyp9p39ze1.jpeg)

### Genre: Mystery · Crime · Psychological Thriller

*Information:*  
*Nine Puzzle* follows a brilliant criminal profiler who becomes involved in a mysterious murder case related to her past. I find it very interesting because it keeps me guessing and makes me think about what’s really going on. The story has a lot of twists and mind games that make every episode exciting. It’s the kind of drama that challenges your thinking and keeps you hooked until the end.

*Main Cast:* Kim Da-mi · Jeon Seong-woo 

*📺 Episodes:* 10

---

## 🌷 4. Pyramid Game
![pyramid game poster](https://www.allkpop.com/upload/2024/01/content/292112/1706580723-pyramid-game-teaser-poster-01.jpeg)

### Genre: School · Psychological · Thriller

*Information:*  
*Pyramid Game* is set in an all-girls school where students are ranked, and the lowest-ranked gets bullied. I like it because the plot is unique and shows how cruel social pressure can be among students. It’s entertaining but also deep since it talks about courage, fear, and standing up for yourself. The drama made me realize how silence can also hurt people who are already struggling.

*Main Cast:* Bona (Kim Ji-yeon) · Jang Da-ah · Ryu Da-in · Kang Na-eon  

*📺 Episodes:* 10
